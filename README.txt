Bring your BLUETTI power station into Homey. See its battery level, solar and grid input, power use and remaining time, switch the AC and DC outputs, and use it all in Flows. Changes made on the power station or in the BLUETTI app show up in Homey within seconds, and an optional Bluetooth fallback keeps the readings coming, and the outputs switchable, when Wi-Fi or the BLUETTI cloud is down.

This is a community app, not made or supported by BLUETTI, and tested with the Elite 30 V2. Your power station must be set up on Wi-Fi with the BLUETTI app. Results with other models, questions and details are welcome in the Homey Community topic.
